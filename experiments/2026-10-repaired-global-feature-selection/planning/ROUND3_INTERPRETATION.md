remain in the family stage. Each family stops at the agreed three-round cap,
not at empirical convergence: every third round expanded the cumulative front.

U.S. equity's trend-free absolute/normalized-credit branch has six inputs, IC
0.325 and Sharpe 1.456596, but worse NLL than competing fronts and slightly worse
than the same-family all-input comparator. A higher IC is not proof of reduced
predictive uncertainty. Annual skip momentum and intermediate momentum still
resist deletion/substitution in the tested compact contexts.

International equity retains compact ten-input risk/oil points with NLL around
-1.842 and a broader sixteen-input point with IC 0.261111/Sharpe 1.474843. Removing
gold/oil ratio from that broad no-HY context worsens every objective in every
seed. A blanket ratio deletion is not supported by the repaired long-history data.

U.S.-FI oil/rate plus lag raises IC 0.038889 in all seeds and aggregate Sharpe
0.011699, but worsens NLL 0.030299. This reverses the ranking effect found when lag
was added to a different no-oil rate parent. The no-lag oil/rate and simplified
credit/volatility models remain separate fronts. Removing genuine nominal/TIPS
state from the oil branch is worse on all three objectives in every seed.

International-FI's eleven-input density-branch simplification improves its parent
in every objective in every seed. The highest-IC eleven-input point has IC 0.4375,
with a Sharpe tradeoff against the twelve-input parent. Replacing annual momentum
with skip momentum is worse on every objective in every seed. Exact mathematical
overlap and broad economic similarity are not sufficient to equate feature roles.

28 family frontier configurations. Its immutable checkpoint is

interpretation and initial actual joint hypotheses are in

for those actual 15-task evaluations. No production changes are authorized here.
