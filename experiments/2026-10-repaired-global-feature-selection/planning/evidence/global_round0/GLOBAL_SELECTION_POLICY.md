# From component evidence to actual joint-model selection

This is the decision protocol for the still-unfinished global stage. It does not
declare a universally important feature set or choose candidates from inclusion
frequency. Concrete masks are preregistered only after complete family evidence.

## Evidence consumed

Use final complete-three-seed family fronts, exact feature lists, paired feature
contexts, explicit adaptive block effects, seed dispersion and monthly/family
calibration reports. Keep both compact and broader competing representations.
An untested input/family pair is unknown, not irrelevant. Contradictory contexts
remain contradictory; repeated nested masks do not become independent votes.

The initial joint all-input control is already evaluated, not synthesized from
family scores. It has pooled IC 0.108780, NLL -2.141413 and conventional portfolio
Sharpe 1.127881, but U.S.-FI within-family IC -0.097222. Most allocated capital
goes to equities. This motivates reporting family outcomes, not a new hidden
family veto or an average of family Sharpes.

## Initial joint batch: at most six novel masks

Span the following hypotheses using exact, disclosed source configurations:

1. A deliberately compact shared-core hypothesis. Treat this as a falsifiable
   representation choice, not the proven intersection of family necessities.
2. A literal selected-family union, preserving useful exceptions without claiming
   the union must be better. State which Pareto representatives were selected and why.
3. A reduced union motivated by tested pruning and documented representation
   redundancy. Exact algebraic identities do not guarantee finite-budget GP invariance.
4. A compact core with rate/local-momentum exceptions suggested by fixed income.
5. A compact core with risk/FX/commodity exceptions suggested by international tasks.
6. A useful matched representation or lag contrast, chosen from the final component
   evidence, so the initial batch is not only a set of unrelated multi-axis changes.

Every retained feature is available to every task in the existing 15-task GP.
An exception here is an input added to the shared mask, not a task mask, zeroed
column, separate family forecast, or new kernel architecture. Fit all three seeds
with the frozen dates, controls and data hashes.

## Adaptive joint loop: at most three rounds, six novel masks per round

After each complete batch, hosted Codex interprets the actual joint outcomes and
preregisters explicit parent-based hypotheses. Exploit promising sparse or broad
branches, explore unresolved cross-family mechanisms, and use exact additions,
removals or substitutions to distinguish explanations. Do not blindly repeat the
family winners or a predetermined parameter grid. Deterministic registration
rejects duplicate masks and enforces source/data/configuration controls.

Use the unchanged three-objective Pareto front and fixed-reference hypervolume.
Retain real new tradeoffs even if one objective worsens. Stop after two consecutive
adaptive rounds without a new objective region or exact-objective smaller
simplification, or after the three-round cap. Do not expand the budget because an
interesting direction remains, and do not count an initial comparison as a fourth
adaptive round. The calibrated optimizer and new formal holdout program remain deferred.

## Interpretation and handoff

Report actual joint portfolio Sharpe and pooled IC/NLL alongside within-family
IC/NLL, allocation exposure, coverage and forecast width. A sleeve Sharpe is only
attribution when it has a full active path; it is not an independent family-model
backtest. Return/RMSE are recorded only, turnover is excluded, and input count
and width are not hidden fourth/fifth objectives. Lower width without adequate
calibration is not lower uncertainty in the useful sense.

All findings are conditional on the finite-budget rank-two research GP, task
sharing, latest-vintage repaired panel and reused score dates. Differences can
reflect kernel geometry and optimization as well as redundant information. Do
not attribute every family gap solely to noisy inputs, claim untouched confirmation,
or silently deploy a selected mask into the differently configured production GP.
